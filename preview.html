The mathematical distinction

Ask what changes the action, not merely the forecast.

Predict plausible answers → update conditional outcome estimates → compute the best decision for each answer → average those best-decision payoffs → subtract the value of deciding now and the cost of asking.

Research question

Can a tabular foundation model make information acquisition more data-efficient — especially when historical observations are few and questions are expensive?

Value of sample information (EVSI) is an established technique. The novel hypothesis worth testing is whether TabPFN helps in scarce-data interactive decision settings.
